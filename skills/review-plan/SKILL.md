# Review Plan

Read the supplied approved design or scope, the bounded plan target, and only explicitly justified supporting files, under the finding rules and verdict in `review-change`. The supplied design and scope own requirements; implementation code supplies justified supporting evidence.

## Plan-Specific Checks

- First real result: the thin business path comes early; each disputed pre-cutover check names the concrete loss it prevents or the operation's concrete prerequisite, otherwise it moves after cutover or is reused.
- Two verification levels: binding outcome or boundary acceptance at the delivery endpoint is distinct from revisable candidate local proof methods; a simulation harness, container rebuild, or duplicate assertion that deterministic checks plus real-path verification already cover is a finding to collapse.
- Milestone and tasks: the milestone is coherent; task IDs, surface inventories, and recovery paths appear where independent work or a risky transition needs them; the executor may revise the plan's granularity, dependency edges, and local proof methods within user-fixed intent.
- Authority: external, destructive, and side-effecting actions keep explicit authority; report genuinely uncovered authority and identify generatable products as executor work.
- Initial readiness and delegation: the first useful local action or parallel assignments are decidable from the plan; a proposed delegated slice is independent, isolated, conflict-free, and convergent; worker counts, fixed topology, exploration prerequisites, or literal dispatch fields are flagged when imposed absent a useful result or real dependency; model and provider selection belongs to the host.
- Essential inputs: already-known inputs for actions through delivery are recorded or recoverable from accessible owner references; a claim that access or credentials were supplied requires a usable reference. Request protected references in place of secret values.
- Live plan: effective goals, authorized discretion, ownership, endpoints, and real pause conditions stay compact; user decisions, investigable facts, generatable products, and technical verification stay distinct.
- Briefs: a worker brief preserves the parent goal, protected behavior, tradeoff boundaries, environment facts, endpoint, and required versus missing authority; implementation choices stay with the worker and authority stays with the parent.
