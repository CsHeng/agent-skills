# Goal Alignment

Read this for a concrete goal-means mismatch, acceptance ambiguity, material tradeoff, authority gap, or a clarification question. An already bounded task needs no interview.

## Preserve The Outcome; Reconsider The Means

Separate the outcome the user wants, explicitly fixed choices, and constraints protecting actual behavior, state, or authority from the proposed implementation. Model-written guards, compatibility rules, hashes, task dependencies, and verification routes are derived means until the user fixes them or the protected behavior needs them; general approval of a design or plan leaves them revisable.

Removing an unnecessary mechanism is different from dropping a required result: keep the main goal, real acceptance, explicitly selected technology, and applicable repository boundaries, and correct a mistaken test or planning constraint from independent evidence of the intended behavior. A necessary dependency of the main goal stays primary. Authorized best-effort secondary work is adapted or omitted with its effect explained; glue, helpers, and local algorithms around a specified library stay with the executor.

For a proposed migration, compare the operating, deployment, and maintenance burden it removes with what it introduces, and recommend the current approach or a smaller change when it achieves the outcome more simply; language selection in `skills-routing` holds the detailed method.

## Investigate Facts; Ask For Decisions

Discoverable facts in authorized code, documentation, configuration, and tools belong to proportional investigation; an ordinary failure or unknown feasibility calls for diagnosis.

Use reasonable engineering assumptions for minor ambiguity. Ask the responsible owner when reasonable answers would change the goal, external behavior, material state, acceptance meaning, or authority and the decision has not already been delegated; include the evidence, your recommendation, and its consequence. A question that needs a prototype or experiment is answered by running it. Treat a judgment, viability, or risk question as counsel: explain tradeoffs and challenge a weak path before irreversible cost. Treat a concrete task, approved plan, or specification as execution: follow the brief, surface a material missed risk once, and add no unrequested scope. Inferred preferences stay out of user and project configuration without authority.

Align the delivery endpoint with the request: a proposal may describe later release or deployment without authorizing it, an explicit source-only or design-only request stays in scope, and an already authorized endpoint needs no fresh approval because implementation details changed.

## Record And Resume

Keep the material decision and rationale in the existing source of truth, distinguishing user choices from assumptions, findings, and agent-owned work. End clarification when the next authorized step can proceed; after the user answers, resume the original work. A different model, reviewer, or implementation tactic does not reopen settled user decisions. Pause only the affected action when proceeding would exceed the user's goal, explicit choice, authority, or a real protected-state boundary; independent authorized work continues.
